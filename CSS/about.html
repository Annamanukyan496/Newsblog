Yerevan's resturants

⸻ Amar Restaurant was established in 2019 and has become one of the most stylish dining destinations in Yerevan. The restaurant is known for its modern design, elegant interior, and relaxing atmosphere. Guests can enjoy a wide variety of delicious fusion dishes prepared with fresh ingredients, as well as excellent service and live music. Amar is a perfect place for family dinners, romantic evenings, and special celebrations.
⸻ Lavash Restaurant opened in 2017 and is one of the most popular restaurants in Yerevan. It specializes in traditional Armenian cuisine made from fresh, locally sourced ingredients. Visitors can watch lavash bread being baked in a traditional tonir and enjoy authentic Armenian hospitality. The restaurant combines cultural traditions with modern comfort, creating a memorable dining experience for every guest.
⸻ Khamancha Restaurant is a famous Armenian restaurant that offers visitors the opportunity to experience the country’s rich culinary traditions. The menu features a wide selection of authentic Armenian dishes prepared from high-quality ingredients. Guests can also enjoy live folk music and traditional performances while dining in a warm and welcoming atmosphere. It is an excellent place to discover Armenian culture and cuisine.
⸻ Zen Restaurant is a contemporary restaurant that specializes in Asian and Japanese cuisine. It is known for its elegant interior, peaceful atmosphere, and beautifully presented dishes. The menu includes fresh sushi, seafood, noodles, and a variety of signature meals prepared by experienced chefs. Zen provides a perfect setting for both casual dining and special occasions.
⸻ Sherep Restaurant opened in 2017 and quickly became one of Yerevan’s most popular restaurants. It is famous for its impressive open kitchen, where guests can watch chefs prepare meals. The restaurant offers a creative selection of Armenian and international dishes, combining traditional flavors with modern cooking techniques. Its stylish interior and professional service make every visit enjoyable.
⸻ Como Restaurant is a modern restaurant that offers a combination of European and international cuisine in a comfortable and elegant setting. The menu includes freshly prepared pasta, meat dishes, seafood, salads, and delicious desserts. Known for its friendly service and relaxing atmosphere, Como is a great place for business lunches, family gatherings, and evenings with friends.
⸻ Ararat Tavern opened in 2019 and is a popular destination for those who want to experience authentic Armenian hospitality. The restaurant serves a wide variety of traditional Armenian and Georgian dishes prepared according to classic recipes. Guests can enjoy live music, a cozy traditional interior, and a welcoming atmosphere that reflects the rich culture of Armenia.
⸻ Loona Restaurant is one of Yerevan’s stylish dining destinations, offering Italian, European, and international cuisine. The restaurant features a modern interior, comfortable seating, and a warm atmosphere suitable for any occasion. Its menu includes handcrafted pasta, pizzas, grilled specialties, seafood, desserts, and refreshing drinks. With its excellent service and high-quality food, Loona provides a memorable dining experience for both locals and tourists.
