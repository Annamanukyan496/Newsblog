    Why are they Budget
      Lavash restaurant-Lavash is considered a budget-friendly restaurant because it offers excellent value for money. Although it is not the cheapest restaurant in Yerevan, its prices are reasonable compared to the high quality of its food and service. Guests can enjoy traditional Armenian dishes made with fresh, local ingredients at affordable prices. The restaurant is also known for its generous portions, warm atmosphere, and friendly service, making it a great choice for families, students, tourists, and anyone who wants to experience authentic Armenian cuisine without spending too much. Because visitors receive high-quality food and a memorable dining experience at fair prices, Lavash is often considered a budget-friendly option rather than a luxury restaurant.
      Khamancha restaurant-Khamancha is considered a budget-friendly restaurant because it offers delicious traditional Armenian and regional dishes at reasonable prices. The restaurant provides a comfortable and welcoming atmosphere without being too expensive compared with many other restaurants in Yerevan. Guests can enjoy high-quality food, large portions, and authentic flavors while spending less money.Another reason is that Khamancha has a menu with different price options, so visitors can choose meals according to their budget. It is a good place for families, friends, and tourists who want to experience Armenian cuisine without paying a high price. The combination of affordable prices, tasty food, and a cozy atmosphere makes Khamancha a popular budget-friendly choice.
      Ararat Tavern restaurant-Tavern Yerevan is considered a budget-friendly restaurant because it offers traditional Armenian food at reasonable prices while keeping good quality and service. Guests can enjoy popular Armenian dishes, generous portions, and a cozy atmosphere without spending as much as they would in many high-end restaurants.The restaurant is a good choice for people who want to experience authentic Armenian cuisine on a comfortable budget. Its wide menu allows visitors to choose different dishes depending on their preferences and spending limits. The combination of affordable meals, traditional flavors, and a welcoming environment makes Tavern Yerevan a popular choice among locals and tourists.
      indian Mehak restaurant-Indian Mehak is considered a budget-friendly restaurant because it provides authentic Indian cuisine with different meal options at accessible prices. Visitors can try traditional Indian dishes, including vegetarian and non-vegetarian options, without needing to spend a large amount of money.The restaurant offers a comfortable atmosphere and a variety of dishes, making it suitable for people who want to enjoy Indian food while staying within a reasonable budget. Its menu includes many choices, allowing guests to select meals according to their preferences and budget.
